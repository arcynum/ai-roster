# Training Levels (Ordered Hierarchy)

Training levels are treated as a cumulative hierarchy in the roster engine. A staff member with a higher training level automatically satisfies the requirements for all lower levels.

# Graduate
- **Learning Stage**: Entry level.
- **Capabilities**: Acute roles.
- **Restrictions**: Shorter shifts only: D8, P8, L3, DISCO, and N8.

# Acute
- **Learning Stage**: 1st role learned.
- **Capabilities**: Acute roles.
- **Restrictions**: Cannot perform Resus, Triage, or Shift Coordinator roles.

# Resus
- **Learning Stage**: 2nd role learned.
- **Capabilities**: Acute and Resus roles.
- **Restrictions**: Cannot perform Triage or Shift Coordinator roles.

# Triage
- **Learning Stage**: 3rd role learned.
- **Capabilities**: Acute, Resus, and Triage roles.
- **Restrictions**: Cannot perform Shift Coordinator role.

# Shift Coordinator
- **Learning Stage**: Final role learned.
- **Capabilities**: All roles (Acute, Resus, Triage, and Shift Coordinator).
- **Restrictions**: None.
