# Acute
- First role people learn.
- Can only perform acute role.
- Cannot perform resus, triage or shift coordinator roles.

# Resus
- Second role people learn.
- Can perform acute and resus roles.
- Cannot perform triage or shift coordinator roles.

# Triage
- Third role people learn.
- Can perform acute, resus, and triage roles.
- Cannot perform shift coordinator role.

# Shift Coordinator
- Final role people learn.
- Can perform all roles - acute, resus, triage, and shift coordinator.
